A particle generator continuously spawns particles at the bottom-center of the canvas, shooting them upward with a random spread. Each particle is given a finite life span; once it elapses the particle is removed from the simulation.

The motion of every particle is advanced by the explicit (forward) Euler method, the simplest ODE integrator. With state x (position) and v (velocity) and acceleration a from gravity, one step is:

x(t + dt) = x(t) + dt * v(t)
v(t + dt) = v(t) + dt * a(t)

Note that explicit Euler uses the old velocity to update the position, which is why it is not symplectic and tends to inject energy — fine for a short-lived particle effect, less ideal for long-running systems.
